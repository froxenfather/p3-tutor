# Grade

Read `PREFERENCES.md`, `NOTES.md`, `.claude/skills/teach/references/GRADING.md`, the quiz, its key and the submission, and the lesson if there is one.

1. Give every item a verdict and, where the response shows one, the misconception.
2. Write feedback for this learner, in the way `PREFERENCES.md` asks: why the answer is right or wrong, and one thing to do next. Never just "correct" or "wrong".
3. Write `grades/NNNN-sN.json` exactly as `FORMATS.md` shows.
4. Write a learning record for what the grades show, in the format in `.claude/skills/teach/references/LEARNING-RECORD-FORMAT.md`, and update `NOTES.md`.
5. Finish with a short summary and the one next step.
